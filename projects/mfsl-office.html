M-Tek Fire & Safety Ltd. (RC 1082534) is a premier engineering and safety solutions firm based in Kaduna, Nigeria, specializing in industrial fire protection, high-definition CCTV surveillance, home automation and safety compliance consulting. Its catalogue spans fire, safety, security and solar equipment, backed by sales, supplies, installation, refilling, maintenance, training and consultancy.

Engineered and deployed for the company, MFSL Office is a custom enterprise inventory and management application. It brings stock, sales, customers, payments, revenue insights and administrative documents into one connected workflow, streamlining stock tracking, equipment servicing records and digital administration.

It supports the service division behind fire protection systems supply and installation, automated surveillance integration, routine technical maintenance and HSE compliance training—improving operational oversight for fire-safety equipment inspections, automated stock reconciliation and faster administrative reporting across the service division.
